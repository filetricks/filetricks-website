Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Filetricks ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Filetricks Android application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account email — if you choose to sign in to sync your Pro subscription across devices.
  • Profile information — display name and profile photo, stored locally on your device.
  • Files you process — PDFs, images, and other documents you select within the App.

1.2 Automatically collected information

  • Device information — Android version, device model, and app version (for crash reporting and compatibility).
  • Advertising ID — via Google AdMob, used to show relevant ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics — which tools you use (for improving the App). We do not track which files you process.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process your files. All file processing happens on your device — files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Display relevant ads via Google AdMob (free tier only).
  • Manage your Pro subscription via Google Play Billing.
  • Improve the App and fix bugs.
  • Respond to your support requests.

3. File Processing & Storage

All file processing happens entirely on your device. When you merge PDFs, compress images, extract text via OCR, or use any other tool, your files are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Output files are saved to your device's local storage (typically under Android/data/com.manoj.filetricks/files/). You can delete them at any time from within the App or your device's file manager.

4. OCR and Machine Learning

Our OCR feature uses Google ML Kit, an on-device machine learning library. Text recognition happens entirely on your device — images and PDFs processed via OCR are never uploaded to Google's servers or ours.

5. Advertising

The free version of Filetricks shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

6. In-App Purchases

Filetricks Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment information. All billing information is handled entirely by Google.

7. App Lock (MPIN) and Security

If you enable App Lock, your MPIN is stored securely on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences and Android Keystore. Your MPIN is never transmitted to us and cannot be recovered by us — if you forget it, you'll need to clear the App's data or reinstall.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information except:

  • With Google AdMob (advertising).
  • With Google Play Billing (payments).
  • When required by law — if we receive a valid legal request.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Files you process are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete them from the App or uninstall the App. Account information (if you signed in) is retained until you delete your account.

You can delete your account at any time from within the App's Settings. This will remove your account data from our servers immediately.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent (which may limit App functionality).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at rageelabs@gmail.com.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: rageelabs@gmail.com
  • Developer: Ragee Labs
  • Country: India

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Filetricks Android application.
