About Filetricks

All your file tools. One app.

Filetricks was born out of a simple frustration: why does converting a PDF or compressing an image require uploading personal files to a random website?

We believe that your files are yours. They shouldn't be sent to a server farm somewhere just to be converted. That's why every single tool in Filetricks runs 100% on your device.

Our Mission

To build the fastest, most private, and most beautiful file utility app on Android. No watermarks, no forced sign-ups, and no shady data practices.

Powered by On-Device AI

We use Google ML Kit to power our OCR (Optical Character Recognition). This means you can extract text from images and PDFs instantly, even without an internet connection, and your documents never leave your phone.

Made in India 🇮🇳

Filetricks is proudly designed and developed by a solo indie developer.
